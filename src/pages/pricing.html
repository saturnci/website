FAQs

Do I have to buy credits?

No, instead of credits, SaturnCI offers straightforward pricing in dollars so that you can understand what you're paying for and how much you're paying for it.

Can I self-host SaturnCI?

No. SaturnCI is available only as a hosted service.

What support is included?

Free unlimited 24/7 support by email, phone, video call and text.

Is there a free tier?

No, but we do offer a free trial.
